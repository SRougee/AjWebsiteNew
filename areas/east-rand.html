Accounting Services Across the East Rand

AJ Accounting & Tax serves individuals and businesses throughout the East Rand from its practice in Elspark, Germiston. Areas served include Germiston, Boksburg, Benoni, Brakpan, Springs and surrounding communities.

The practice provides a broad range of accounting and tax services while maintaining a personal approach.

East Rand accounting services

  • Personal income tax returns
  • Business accounting and bookkeeping
  • VAT and VAT201 returns
  • Payroll and EMP201 submissions
  • Financial statements
  • CIPC and tax clearance
  • SARS queries and disputes
