About AJ Accounting & Tax

A local accounting and tax practice in Elspark, Germiston, built around professional expertise, personal attention and dependable service.

Meet Alwin Jennings

AJ Accounting & Tax is led by Alwin Jennings, a Chartered Accountant and Registered Tax Practitioner with 20 years of professional accounting and tax experience.

Alwin established the practice in Elspark, Germiston in 2011 with a simple approach: clients should receive professional advice without losing the personal service that comes from knowing who is responsible for their work.

Today, AJ Accounting & Tax assists individuals, families and businesses across Germiston, Boksburg, Benoni, Brakpan, Springs and the wider East Rand and Gauteng. Clients can receive support in person or remotely, with work handled by a closely managed team under Alwin's direction.

A practical, personal approach

Accounting and tax are not only about submitting forms. Good financial support means understanding the client's circumstances, keeping records organised, explaining what matters and dealing with issues before they become bigger problems.

Whether you need an annual ITR12 return, monthly bookkeeping, VAT and payroll support, financial statements or help with a SARS query, the aim is to give you a clear answer and a practical next step.

What clients can expect

  • Direct, personal attention rather than a call-centre experience
  • 20 years of professional accounting and tax experience
  • A closely managed team working under Alwin's direction
  • Clear communication and practical explanations
  • Professional attention to accuracy, deadlines and compliance
  • Services tailored to individuals, families and owner-managed businesses
