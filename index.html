About the firm

Local, personal accounting — without the agency mark-up

AJ Accounting & Tax was founded by Alwin Jennings in Elspark, Germiston in 2011. With two decades of expertise in taxation and accounting, Alwin has guided countless individuals and businesses through every tax challenge with confidence and precision.
