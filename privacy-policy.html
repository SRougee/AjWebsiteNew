Privacy Policy

How AJ Accounting & Tax collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use this website or contact the practice.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AJ Accounting & Tax, based in Elspark, Germiston, South Africa, handles personal information collected through this website, including information submitted through our contact form and information generated through website analytics.

We aim to process personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African privacy laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). 2. Information you provide to us

When you use the contact form, you may provide your full name, email address, telephone number, the service you are enquiring about, and the contents of your message. These details are used to understand your enquiry, contact you, provide information or prepare an appropriate response or quotation.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the general website contact form unless it is specifically requested and a suitable secure method has been provided.

3. Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager/Google Analytics technology. The website is configured with the Google Analytics measurement ID G-EV2E8FYEG5. Analytics may collect technical and usage information such as pages visited, approximate location, device/browser information, referral source, interaction events and other information used to understand how visitors use the website and improve its performance.

Analytics information is generally used in an aggregated or statistical way and is not used by AJ Accounting & Tax to identify visitors personally from ordinary website usage. Google may process analytics information on its own systems under its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics services may use cookies or similar identifiers to distinguish visits and understand how the website is used. Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies, although doing so can affect some website functionality or the accuracy of analytics.

5. Why we process information

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal information to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective or existing clients, provide accounting and tax services, administer client relationships, meet legal or regulatory obligations, protect the website and business, and understand website usage for improvement.

POPIA permits processing in circumstances including consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations and legitimate interests, provided the applicable requirements are met. citeturn670499search1

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers where necessary to operate the website, receive and process enquiries, provide technology or analytics services, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law. Where third-party services are used, their own privacy terms may also apply.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal and professional obligations. POPIA requires records not to be retained longer than necessary, subject to lawful exceptions. citeturn670499search3

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where lawful, withdraw consent where consent is the relevant basis for processing, and object to certain processing.
